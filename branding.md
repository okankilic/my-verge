# Wishcrumbs --- Brand Foundation & Identity Brief

> **Working brand guide · Version 1.0 · November 2026**\
> **Brand:** Wishcrumbs\
> **Core tagline:** Your life, piece by piece.\
> **Brand idea:** Little things make a life.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand at a glance

**Wishcrumbs** is a personal life companion for collecting the things
you want to do, turning intentions into action, and keeping the memories
that come from living them.

It brings together:

-   **Wishes:** things you dream about or want to experience.
-   **Tasks:** practical things that need doing, from paying a bill to
    making an appointment.
-   **Plans:** the preparation and steps that help an intention happen.
-   **Experiences:** the things you actually do.
-   **Memories:** the moments and stories you want to keep.

Wishcrumbs should feel like a place for **life**, not another demanding
productivity system.

### Brand essence

**A collection of little intentions, actions and moments that together
make a life.**

### One-sentence description

Wishcrumbs helps you collect what you want to do, make it happen, and
keep the moments along the way.

### Short description

A home for your wishes, plans, everyday tasks and life memories --- all
in one personal place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Meaning behind the name

The name combines **Wish** and **Crumbs**.

-   **Wish** represents possibility: something you want, hope for, try,
    experience or achieve.
-   **Crumbs** represents the little pieces: small actions, everyday
    details, discoveries and memories.

The metaphor is that a life is not made only of huge milestones. It is
made of many little things --- some planned, some spontaneous, some
ordinary and some unforgettable.

A crumb can be: - A small task you need to finish. - A place you want to
visit. - A skill you want to learn. - A concert you want to attend. - A
meal you want to try. - A moment you want to remember.

Together, those crumbs form a personal trail through life.

### Important interpretation

Do not position "crumbs" as insignificant leftovers. In the Wishcrumbs
world, every crumb matters. Small things are not secondary to big
dreams; they are part of the same life.

## 3. Positioning

### Category

A personal life organiser and memory companion.

### Positioning statement

For people who want to make more of life and remember what matters,
Wishcrumbs is a personal space to collect intentions, organise everyday
tasks, turn plans into experiences and preserve memories. Unlike a
traditional to-do list or a simple wishlist, Wishcrumbs connects what
you want to do, what you are doing and what you have done.

### What Wishcrumbs is

-   Personal, not corporate.
-   Broad, not limited to shopping wishlists or bucket lists.
-   Encouraging, not demanding.
-   Useful for both ordinary tasks and extraordinary experiences.
-   A bridge between future intentions, present action and past
    memories.

### What Wishcrumbs is not

-   Only a wishlist.
-   Only a task manager.
-   Only a habit tracker.
-   Only a journal or photo album.
-   A competitive productivity tool that makes people feel behind.

### Core product principle

**A wish can be anything you want to do.**

It can be "pay the electricity bill", "read a book", "learn Spanish",
"visit Peru" or "see my favourite band live". The product should not
imply that only ambitious or exciting goals deserve a place.

## 6. Voice and tone

### Voice principles

1.  **Encouraging:** invite people to take a step; never scold them.
2.  **Human:** sound like a thoughtful companion, not a
    project-management dashboard.
3.  **Clear:** use familiar words and short sentences.
4.  **Positive:** focus on possibility and progress.
5.  **Non-judgmental:** unfinished wishes are not failures.

### Preferred language

Use: - Add a crumb - Save this for later - Make a plan - Take the next
step - Mark it as done - Keep this memory - Look how far you have come

Avoid: - Crush your goals - Maximise productivity - You are falling
behind - Failed task - Overdue dreams - Optimise every minute

### Sample interface copy

  Situation          Suggested copy
  ------------------ -------------------------------------
  Empty home         What would you like to do?
  Add an item        Add a crumb
  New wish           What have you been wanting to do?
  Planning           Let's make it happen
  Completed item     You made it happen!
  Save a memory      Keep this moment
  No current plans   There is always room for a new idea
  Review             Your life, piece by piece

### Writing conventions

-   Use sentence case, not title case, for most interface labels.
-   Prefer concise verbs.
-   Use contractions where they sound natural.
-   Avoid excessive exclamation marks.
-   Do not force the word "crumb" into every sentence. Use it as a
    recognisable brand device, not a verbal gimmick.

## 7. Visual identity direction

### Overall creative direction

**Warm modern minimalism with playful, tactile details.**

The identity should feel like a beautifully organised personal
collection: clean enough for everyday use, warm enough to feel human,
and distinctive enough to be recognisable.

Think: - Soft, warm backgrounds. - A confident, friendly wordmark. -
Rounded but not overly bubbly shapes. - Small "crumb" dots or clustered
shapes as a visual motif. - A restrained palette with one recognisable
accent. - Plenty of breathing room.

Avoid: - Generic productivity-app blue. - Corporate gradients and
abstract tech symbols. - Literal bread, toast or bakery imagery as the
main identity. - Overly childish cartoon illustrations. - Excessive
decoration that competes with the content.

### Logo concept

**Primary logo:** lowercase wordmark `wishcrumbs`.

Recommended characteristics: - Friendly, rounded sans-serif lettering. -
Clear legibility at small sizes. - Slightly distinctive letterforms,
especially `w`, `s` or `b`. - A custom detail in one letter can make the
wordmark ownable. - Keep the wordmark simple; let the icon carry the
crumb metaphor.

**Icon concept:** a small cluster of three to five rounded pieces,
arranged as a trail, constellation or gently growing shape.

The icon should suggest: - Individual pieces becoming a whole. - A path
or trail through life. - A collection that can keep growing.

Do not make the icon look like a bakery logo, food delivery app or
generic checklist.

### Logo system to develop

1.  Primary horizontal wordmark.
2.  Compact wordmark for narrow spaces.
3.  App icon.
4.  Single-colour version.
5.  Light and dark background versions.
6.  Small-size version tested at 16, 24 and 32 px.

### Colour principles

-   Oat and Cocoa create the recognisable foundation.
-   Terracotta is the main accent; use it consistently.
-   Honey, Sage and Lavender add variety for categories or
    illustrations.
-   Forest provides a grounded dark-mode direction.
-   Do not use all accents at equal strength on one screen.
-   Maintain accessible contrast for text, controls and status
    indicators.

### Suggested semantic use

-   Wishes / ideas: Lavender.
-   Plans / preparation: Honey.
-   Active / in progress: Terracotta.
-   Completed / experiences: Sage.
-   Memories: Forest or a warm neutral treatment.

These are optional product associations, not fixed meanings. Test them
with users.

## 9. Typography

### Direction

Use a friendly, contemporary sans-serif family with excellent screen
legibility.

Suggested font candidates to evaluate: - **Plus Jakarta Sans** ---
modern, warm and distinctive. - **Nunito Sans** --- friendly and
rounded. - **DM Sans** --- clean and versatile. - **Inter** --- highly
legible and practical.

### Recommended hierarchy

-   Display / campaign: expressive weight, generous spacing.
-   Screen titles: semibold.
-   Body: regular, comfortable line height.
-   Metadata: smaller but still accessible.
-   Buttons: medium or semibold.

Avoid overly rounded display fonts for long passages. The brand can be
playful without sacrificing usability.

## 10. Graphic language and illustration

### Crumb motif

Build a flexible visual system from small rounded shapes:

-   A short trail of dots.
-   Three or four pieces forming a larger symbol.
-   A cluster that expands as more items are collected.
-   Small shapes that connect to form a path.
-   Scattered pieces used sparingly as decorative punctuation.

The motif can represent both an individual item and the larger
collection.

### Illustration style

-   Simple, warm, editorial illustrations.
-   Rounded geometry and soft organic forms.
-   Human activities and everyday objects.
-   Small details that reward attention.
-   Limited colours from the brand palette.

Use illustrations to support meaning, not to fill empty space.

### Photography

When photography is used: - Prefer authentic everyday moments over
staged perfection. - Show varied scales of life: a cup of coffee, a
book, a train journey, a concert, a shared meal. - Use natural light and
warm, believable colour. - Avoid luxury-only or achievement-only
imagery. Ordinary life is part of the story.

## 11. Product language and experience model

The product experience can be organised around a simple progression:

### Dream → Ready → Go

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Stage                   User meaning            Product role
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Dream                   I want to do this       Capture the idea or
                                                  intention

  Ready                   How can I make it       Plan, prepare and break
                          happen?                 it down

  Go                      I'm doing it / I've     Act, complete and
                          done it                 record the experience
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

After the experience, the user can preserve a memory. This creates a
natural connection between the future, present and past.

### Suggested product vocabulary

  Concept                             Preferred term
  ----------------------------------- -------------------
  A saved intention or item           Crumb
  Collection of saved items           My crumbs
  A group of related items            Collection
  Steps needed to achieve something   Plan / steps
  Completed experience                Done / experience
  A saved reflection or record        Memory
  Overall history                     My journey

Use standard terms when clarity matters. Do not invent unfamiliar
terminology for basic functions.

## 13. Brand do's and don'ts

### Do

-   Treat everyday tasks and big dreams as equally valid.
-   Make the brand feel personal and welcoming.
-   Use the crumb metaphor with restraint and consistency.
-   Celebrate progress without turning life into a competition.
-   Make room for spontaneity as well as planning.
-   Keep the visual system simple, recognisable and accessible.

### Don't

-   Position the product as only a bucket list.
-   Make it sound like a shopping wishlist.
-   Suggest users must be constantly productive.
-   Use food or bakery imagery as the central brand idea.
-   Overuse crumbs, trails and puns in interface copy.
-   Promise that every wish will come true.

## 15. Brand north star

When making a design, product or marketing decision, ask:

1.  Does this help people collect something they genuinely want to do or
    remember?
2.  Does it make everyday life feel as welcome as extraordinary
    experiences?
3.  Does it encourage action without creating pressure?
4.  Does it feel personal, warm and clear?
5.  Does it reinforce the idea that little things make a life?

If the answer is yes, it is probably right for Wishcrumbs.
